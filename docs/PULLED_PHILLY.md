with closures removed (see "Closures checked"). Philadelphia has **no Michelin
guide** (not a Michelin region), so the backbone is local + national editorial
(Infatuation, Philadelphia Magazine/Foobooz, Time Out, NYT, Eater, the Inquirer's
Craig LaBan, PUNCH) plus the James Beard Awards. Six restaurant lists, four bar
lists — strong coverage.

## Consensus leaders
All six restaurant lists have deep overlap: **Kalaya, Mawn, Royal Sushi & Izakaya,
Friday Saturday Sunday, Zahav, My Loup** appear on 5–6 lists; **Honeysuckle,
Meetinghouse, Vernick, Illata, Ambra, Mish Mish, Provenance, La Jefa/Tequilas,
Vetri** on 3–4. Mawn + Meetinghouse carry the NYT national credit. Very buildable.
