# The Research Triangle (Raleigh–Durham–Chapel Hill) — pulled lists (Nov 14 2026)

A real food region without the usual national-guide backbone: **no Michelin, no Eater edition, no NYT city guide, no Infatuation.** The authorities here are local/regional — INDY Week, Axios Raleigh, the News & Observer (Greg Cox), Walter Magazine — plus a **deep James Beard: Southeast** record (Ashley Christensen/Poole's won Outstanding Restaurateur; Andrea Reusing of Lantern and Matt Kelly of Mateo each won Best Chef: Southeast; Ricky Moore of Saltbox won an America's Classics award; Brewery Bhavana was an Outstanding Bar finalist). JB will carry the "national" tier for this city.

Note: this is a 3-city metro — tag venues by city (Raleigh / Durham / Chapel Hill / Carrboro / Cary) rather than neighborhood.
